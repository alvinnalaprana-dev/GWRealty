How it works

Five simple steps for every lease

Staff only take photos and tick items on their phone. Saving, comparing and making the report happen automatically.

  1. Once per villa

    List the villa

    We record everything in the villa one time, with a photo of each spot.

  2. Staff

    Tenant moves in

    Staff walk through the villa, take photos and check every item.

  3. Tenant

    Tenant agrees

    The tenant signs on the phone, or replies to a link within 3 days.

  4. Staff

    Tenant moves out

    Staff check again. The system shows only what changed.

  5. GW Realty

    You decide

    For each change: no charge, repair, or take from the deposit.

The report

A familiar report, made automatically

  • Villa, tenant, lease dates, staff name and report number at the top
  • Each area with its photo and item list; at move-out, the move-in photo sits beside it
  • A last page listing the changes for GW Realty to decide on
  • Tenant and staff signatures with date and time
  • In English and Indonesian, with the GW Realty letterhead

Photos you can trust

Quick for staff, strong as proof

Camera or gallery

Photos are made smaller on the phone before sending, so it stays fast even on mobile data.

Originals kept

Full-size photos are saved in a separate folder. GW Realty clears it whenever it wants.

Dates checked

Gallery photos older than 24 hours are marked "old photo". The send time is recorded by the system.

Reports locked

Nothing can be changed after sending. A correction makes a new version and keeps the old one.
