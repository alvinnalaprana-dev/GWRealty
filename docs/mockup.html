1Take photos

Staff only take photos

At the villa, staff photograph each room. Or they take the photos first and upload them later from the office, from the phone's gallery.

No typing. Photos are grouped by room. A photo taken on an earlier day gets a small warning, so nobody uses an old picture by mistake.

Staff
2AI makes the list · Manager checks

AI reads the photos and builds the page. The manager checks it and approves.

AI lists every item it sees in each room, with the count and condition. The manager adds the villa details, corrects anything AI got wrong, and approves.

3Saved + reminder

Everything is saved, and the check-out date is remembered

Once approved, the photos, the list and the villa details are saved, and a check-in report is made. The system sends a reminder before the check-out date, for example 7 days before and the day before.

4The page

A clean check-in report, made automatically

After approving, the manager presses Generate PDF. The report shows each room with its photos and the approved list. It is saved to the villa record and can be printed, sent to the tenant, or signed at the villa.

5Take photos again

Staff open the same villa record and take new photos

The check-in photo is shown for each room, so staff can take the new photo from the same angle. Again, photos only, live or uploaded later.

Staff
6AI compares · Manager approves

AI checks the new photos against the check-in list

AI goes through the approved check-in list and marks what it still sees, what is missing and what looks damaged. The manager confirms or corrects each change and approves the check-out report.
