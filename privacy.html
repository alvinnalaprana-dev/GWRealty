Privacy Policy

GW Realty Villa Inventory · Last updated 25 October 2026

GW Realty Villa Inventory ("the app", at gwrealty.netlify.app) is an internal tool used by GW Realty and its staff to record the contents and condition of rental villas at check-in and check-out. The app is built and operated for GW Realty by Kriyator.

What information the app handles

  • Staff and manager accounts: name, email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Inventory records: villa, rooms, items, quantities, conditions, notes, dates and photos of the rooms taken by staff.
  • Tenant details (optional): name, phone, email, passport or ID number and notes, entered by the manager when needed for the lease.

How it is used

The information is used only to prepare, approve and print check-in and check-out reports, to compare a villa's condition between the two, and to remind the manager by email seven days before a check-out. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Google Drive and Google Sheets (the operator's own Google account) store the records, photos and PDF reports. GW Realty has view access to the photos and reports.
  • Gmail (the operator's own account) sends the check-out reminder to the GW Realty manager.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) receives room photos to list the items it can see. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
  • Netlify hosts the web pages; an automation server run by the operator connects the app to the services above.

Use of Google user data

The app connects only to the operator's own Google account, with that account holder's permission, to create and read the inventory files, update the records sheet and send reminder emails. It does not access any other Google user's data. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide these features, is not shared with third parties except as described above, and its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see it

Only signed-in GW Realty staff and managers. Staff see villas and inventory lists; tenant contact details are visible to managers only. Access is checked on the server for every request.

Keeping and deleting data

Records and photos are kept as evidence for the lease and its deposit settlement. Photos removed in the app are kept in Google Drive until the folder owner deletes them. To ask for a correction or deletion, contact us at the address below.
